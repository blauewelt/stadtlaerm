- 08:33 — lfFlutterDb detrends the 125 ms LF level with a centred running MEDIAN (±8 ticks, symmetric, shrinking at the edges) instead of a moving average: with the moving average every abrupt on/offset counted as flutter and a plain 2 s 1 kHz tone burst on noise came out as wind (≈ 8 dB); the median keeps steps and slow trends and leaves the back-and-forth fluctuation. Consequence: a single sub-second thump in a short event gives little flutter and is caught by lfShare (if LF-only).
