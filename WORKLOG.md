- 14:29 — DEVIATION (manifest): the prompt says "add INTERNET, nothing else". But WorkManager (`androidx.work:work-runtime:2.9.1`, the AAR's manifest) merges `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` and `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`, and the framework's JobScheduler (`JobSchedulerService$JobSchedulerStub`, android-all 15, compat change 271850009) throws `SecurityException: ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE required for jobs with a connectivity constraint`. «nur über WLAN» (NetworkType.UNMETERED) and even "any network" (CONNECTED) are connectivity constraints, so the public flavour must also get `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (a normal, install-time permission: lets the app see whether a network is up and metered; no location, no Wi-Fi names — `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE` stays removed). `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` stays removed (`tools:node="remove"`): WorkManager uses it only to reschedule after a reboot, and with nothing measured before the app is opened again there is nothing to send; the next app start reschedules. Documented in PRIVACY.md's permission list.

- 14:55 — Upload window: DESIGN §4.6 says "all minutes and events newer than the last acknowledged start", but §2.3 says off means off. Chosen: at the very first opt-in the floor is now − 7 days (the server refuses older records anyway, §8 `MAX_RECORD_AGE_DAYS`), said on the explanation screen; after a later off→on, delete, or «Neue Kennung» the floor is "now", so nothing measured while sharing was off is ever sent. A 1-hour margin keeps records that would expire in transit from being sent.
- 14:55 — Batch boundary: two minute rows can share a start (restart within one minute). Markers are "start > marker", so a full batch is cut before a group of equal starts (one row of look-ahead) and the group goes with the next batch. Caught and fixed a bug in my first version (it always dropped the last row of a full batch) before the tests ran.
- 14:55 — `rejected` handling: refused records are remembered by (kind, start) in the upload state (bounded to 2000 entries, total count kept) and filtered out of every later batch; the marker moves over them. The settings screen shows the count. The rejection reason text is kept locally only.
- 14:55 — Re-evaluation: after «Alte Messungen neu bewerten» completes, markers are reset so the last 7 days are re-sent with the new levels (server replaces by start, §4.3). Hooked by observing `Recalibrator.state` in `UploadModule.start`, so no change to the measurement or re-evaluation code. The upload after a measurement stops is hooked the same way (LiveState running → stopped, job delayed 15 s so the service's final minute is in the DB) — DESIGN §9 "nothing in the measurement path changes" kept literally.
- 14:55 — Rate limit: the server allows 60 requests/device/hour; a run makes at most 24 requests and schedules a follow-up 30 min later if more is waiting (e.g. after a week offline: 7 days ≈ 8 minute batches + event batches, fits in one run anyway).
